A title can be a string or explicit nonempty text lines. Paragraphs/points are arrays of nonempty text. Cards accept title and/or description, with at least one nonempty; absent fields remove their own spacing. Agenda items accept title, optional description, and either a positive integer page or target slide ID, not both. Target pages resolve from actual ordered IDs after reordering; missing targets block export. Manual page labels are explicit source choices, not automatic references.

Every single-image entry outside Gallery declares pairedTemplate pointing to a same-family no-image recipe. Registry initialization rejects a missing pair. Image-free cover/split/agenda use their designed larger text regions; cards drop the photo and use readable ink footers; the revised section opener follows the supplied image-free Figma arrangement. The figure alternative cannot silently discard a source diagram: source coverage still requires every provided visual unless the source/brief is deliberately revised with authorization.

## Preview and actual instances

materialize is preview-only and clones example content/assets. instantiate starts from layout/recipe override defaults and explicitly supplied content; it never inherits sample copy, photo, logo, section, footer, or date. validateContract checks structural compatibility; BriteEngine.prepare checks actual fonts/assets, safe areas, line budgets, and collisions. The compose helper validates before saving, and the exporter blocks invalid geometry.

Unknown content fields are rejected so supplied text cannot disappear. Common legacy fields are logo and section; common chrome is supported on all layouts and at deck scope. ## Overrides and fit

Native canvas is 1920 × 1080, with safe content y100–978. Default positioning may be more specific to a composition. Allowed title overrides are fontSize 32–220, width 160 up to the selected recipe's title region, lineHeight .8–1.6, integer targetLines 1–8, and align left/center/right. Group overrides are verticalAlign top/center/bottom and offsetY −300 to 300. Explicit targetLines replaces the default title-line ceiling; geometric checks still apply.

These bounds do not authorize automatic shrinking or guarantee fit. The renderer tries default spacing and one compact retry, then reports a conflict. Source engineering ceilings are separate. Preserve wording and use permitted continuation or another compatible template when necessary. Omitting optional content removes its gap; hiding common chrome does not relocate the remaining chrome slots or expand the safe region.
